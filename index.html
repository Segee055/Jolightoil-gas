WHO ARE WE

THE JOLIGHT OIL & GAS LTD is a Nigerian energy company specialising in the supply and distribution of quality diesel to businesses, industries, and organisations. Our services include diesel supply and delivery, providing reliable fuel solutions tailored to our customers’ needs.

We serve clients across Africa, with a commitment to timely delivery, competitive pricing, safety, and customer satisfaction. Our goal is to become one of Africa’s leading diesel suppliers, powering businesses and supporting economic growth.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, timely, and high-quality diesel supply to businesses and industries across Africa, ensuring uninterrupted operations through efficient distribution, competitive pricing, and a commitment to safety and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To be the leading diesel supply and other petroleum products distribution companies, recognised for reliability, operational excellence, strong customer relationships, and a commitment to powering businesses and driving economic growth in Africa.
